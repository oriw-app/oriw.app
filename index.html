Frame · Security

Private by design, and you can check

Boards are plaintext only in memory while unlocked. Everything on disk, in backups and on the wire is encrypted, and the code is open for anyone to audit.

Frame · FAQ

Questions

Is Oriw really free?

Yes. The desktop app is open source under Apache-2.0 and free forever, with unlimited boards. Companies pay only for enterprise features like SSO and a support agreement, or for the optional hosted relay.

Where are my boards stored?

On your computer, inside an encrypted vault. If you turn on Google Drive backup, Oriw encrypts each board before upload, so Google stores only files it cannot read.

Which AI models can I use?

Claude with your own API key, or Llama running locally through Ollama. With a local model, nothing leaves your device. You can also turn AI off completely.

Can my team work on the same board?

Live sessions arrive in 0.2: share an invite link and edit together, end-to-end encrypted. A self-hosted team server follows in 0.3.

How do you say Oriw?

OR-ee-w. It's a made-up word, so it means whatever you build on it.

Can I bring my existing boards?

An importer for files you export from other whiteboards is on the roadmap. Oriw's own format is open and documented, so your boards are never locked in.
